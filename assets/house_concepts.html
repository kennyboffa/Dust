House / Building Art Style Concepts

5 different art styles for wasteland buildings. Each at 4x scale, isometric perspective.

Style A: Chunky Pixel Dithered (Current)

Low-res with ordered dithering. Rough adobe/wood construction. Fallout 2 pre-rendered feel.

Style B: Clean Flat Pixel

Clean flat colors with bold outlines. GBA/SNES RPG style. Clear readable shapes, vibrant palette.

Style C: Dark Silhouette

Moody dark structure with lit windows glowing warm. Limbo/Inside atmospheric style.

Style D: Painterly Textured

Rough brushstroke textures, visible wood grain. Darkest Dungeon hand-crafted look with warm earthy tones.

Style E: Geometric / Low-Poly

Sharp angular shapes, bold flat color panels. Triangle-based construction. Modern indie game aesthetic.
